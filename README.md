Mocapless provides a platform that automatically rigs and animates 3D animal models from simple text descriptions. Users can upload their own 3D models or simply name an animal, and the service generates a fully rigged animation ready for export to Unity, Unreal, Godot, Blender, and other engines. The service offers free trials with two animations, and paid plans for additional characters and downloads. It targets game developers, filmmakers, educators, and product demonstrators needing realistic animal motion without manual rigging.

**Areas:** Animation · 3D · Gaming · AI · Platform
